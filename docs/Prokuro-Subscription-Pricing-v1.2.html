1. Direct answers (why this revision exists)

Yes — enable purchase on Free. There is no good demerit. Blocking buy was an old upgrade wedge. A hardware buyer who cannot try purchasing does not feel the product. Abuse is quote-scraping, not “they placed two Digi-Key orders.” Cap Free at 5 purchasing actions / month (max 2 orders when ordering is on). Paid gets 40 / 200. Flip can_purchase to true on Free. Today’s deploy still has Digi-Key ordering off — Free can use the quote path when it is live; orders inherit the same cap later.

Yes — ship an Enterprise sales plan for OEMs above Scale. v1/v1.1 hid this as “off the public grid.” That reads as “we don’t sell to large companies.” Enterprise is a real fourth offer: custom annual, invoice, SSO, limits above Scale. Not a Stripe self-serve SKU.

We do not delete data. v1 said 14-day Free delete. v1.1 changed the policy to keep-forever but still labeled a table row like “retention,” which looked unchanged. v1.2: there is no retention/delete policy. BOMs and MPN snapshots stay in S3/Dynamo. What differs by tier is how often we refresh (Free weekly, paid daily, Enterprise daily + priority). Extra BOMs stay stored; they just drop out of the monitored set.

3. Free purchase — why there is no demerit

Cost: Digi-Key/Mouser lookups are quota, not cash. Five actions/month cannot move AWS. Conversion: trying a real buy is stronger than a locked button. Support: fewer “why can’t I purchase on Free?” tickets. Remaining risk is scraping — 5 actions + 1 org/domain + work email stops farms. If Free purchasing-action 402s spike with zero analyses, tighten to 3, don’t flip the boolean back off.

4. Enterprise sales plan

Who: 25+ people in procurement/engineering, 200+ live BOMs, or SSO/security review. How sold: website “Contact sales,” not Checkout. Commercial: custom annual, invoice, floor above Scale. Product: everything in Scale plus custom line/BOM/API budgets, daily+priority refresh, higher Bedrock budget, SSO, SLA, DPA, audit log. Implementation: plan=enterprise set by ops/Stripe Billing (invoice) or a flag on the billing Dynamo item — no fourth self-serve price ID. App treats unknown high entitlements as Enterprise, not Free.

5. Data policy (not “retention”)

Keep all account BOMs and all MPN snapshots. Do not TTL-delete for plan or inactivity. Build the component network. Daily job refreshes paid active-BOM MPNs first, then leftover Digi-Key quota for catalog freshness, then Free’s weekly 1-BOM pass. Stale Free UI: banner, data still there. Cancel/downgrade: keep files, drop to Free refresh + 5 purchase actions. S3: expire noncurrent versions at 30 days only (cost hygiene), never the current BOM.

11. 30-day metrics and red flags

Counters

  • Free → paid among Free with ≥1 analysis (target ≥8%)
  • 402s by cap (lines, analyses, purchasing actions)
  • Free purchasing actions with 0 analyses (scrape signal)
  • Stale-banner → Growth checkout
  • Enterprise demo requests vs self-serve
  • AWS bill vs ~$70; Bedrock $; Digi-Key 429s

Red flags

Tighten Free actions immediately if scrape farms, Digi-Key 429s starve paid sync, or bill >$150 with <20 paid. Do not turn can_purchase back off. Raise self-serve price only after 30 days if conversion >25% and paid users >80% of caps. If conversion <5%, tighten Free volume/refresh copy, not dollars.
